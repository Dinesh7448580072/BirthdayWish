📝 WHAT TO EDIT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Edit ONE FILE: src/data/birthdayContent.js

Replace:
  • YOUR_GIRLFRIEND_NAME → Her name
  • YOUR_LOVE_LETTER_PARAGRAPH_1-4 → Your love letter
  • YOUR_CAPTION_1-6 → Photo captions
  • YOUR_SURPRISE_MESSAGE_HERE → Gift box message
  • YOUR_FINAL_MESSAGE_HERE → Closing message
  • Photo paths → Update to your filenames
  • Music path → Your audio file (optional)
